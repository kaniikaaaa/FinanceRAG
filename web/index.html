Method

The query is embedded and matched against the wire by nearest-neighbour search over pgvector. Ranking weighs freshness alongside similarity, so a stale story loses to a current one that fits nearly as well. The three best passages become the model's context. Every answer is composed from those passages alone, each dated and linked.

Answers you can trace back to the wire

Ask a question about the financial news wire. FinanceRAG retrieves the passages that bear on it, prefers what ran most recently, and composes a reply from those passages alone — so nothing here rests on the model's memory.
